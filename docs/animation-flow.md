# Animation flow

This is a motion specification, not an implementation. Major transitions will use physical movement, material cues, and easing rather than fades or instant swaps.

1. **Photobooth idle state:** the machine is present and ready; the printer slot is empty.
2. **Start button interaction:** a physical button press begins the sequence.
3. **Printing state:** internal printer processing begins without a camera-flash interaction.
4. **Photograph emerging from printer slot:** the photo starts to appear from the actual slot.
5. **Photograph fully exiting:** the print clears the slot and settles naturally.
6. **Photograph moving toward album:** the application layer coordinates its path away from the booth.
7. **Album appearing:** the album enters as a physical object in the destination scene.
8. **Photograph being placed into album:** the printed photo is seated on the opening page.
9. **Album opening:** the cover and spine open with depth and shadow.
10. **Main album interaction:** visitors can explore the invitation as an album.
11. **Page turning:** click, keyboard, and swipe inputs drive bent pages and realistic shadows.
12. **Final RSVP experience:** the final album page presents RSVP as the concluding interaction.
