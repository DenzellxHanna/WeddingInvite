# Animation flow

This is a motion specification, not an implementation. Major transitions use physical movement, material cues, depth, and easing rather than fades or instant swaps. This is a photobooth sequence, not a camera-flash sequence.

1. **Photobooth idle:** the physical machine is ready and its printer slot is empty.
2. **Start:** the guest physically presses the booth button.
3. **Processing:** the machine processes the photograph.
4. **Print emergence:** the photograph visibly emerges from the actual printer slot.
5. **Print exit:** the photo clears the machine and settles as a physical print.
6. **Album arrival:** the print transitions toward a newly appearing, closed album.
7. **Cover presentation:** the guest sees the closed `D/H WEDDING` cover.
8. **Photo placement:** the print is placed onto the left page of the first spread.
9. **Album opening:** the cover opens with spine depth and shadows.
10. **Spread 1:** `THE INVITATION` and the `MAIN INVITATION` are revealed.
11. **Spread 2:** `NAVIGATION` and the album-integrated `NAVIGATION MENU` are available.
12. **Spread 3:** `DETAILS` and `WEDDING DETAILS` are revealed.
13. **Spread 4:** `DRESS CODE` and its content are revealed.
14. **Spread 5:** `GIFTS` and its minimal monetary-gift message are revealed.
15. **Spread 6:** `RSVP` and the `RSVP FORM` conclude the experience.

Future page turns will support click, tap, swipe, and appropriate keyboard input with paper bending, shadows, perspective, and realistic easing. The final physical animations are deliberately not implemented in this stage.
